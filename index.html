About

I am the Client Delivery Lead on Kraken Distribution, based in Melbourne. I work with distributor product owners and business owners, and I am their first point of contact when a domain issue needs a clear path through engineering, QA, and solution architecture.

My work is implementations, enhancements, and meter to cash. I write the client need so engineering can build it, and I make sure each change strengthens the platform the whole network runs on.

I came from Octopus Energy in Wellington, the retail side of the same system: affordability, billing access, vulnerable customers, Power BI, and a 28% drop in recurring issues. Distribution is where those bills begin, and I am grateful to be building it.
